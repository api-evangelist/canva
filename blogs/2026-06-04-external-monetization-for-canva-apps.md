This guide explores practical strategies for monetizing Canva applications through external payment methods, recommending Stripe Payment Links as simple links that users can open to pay for one-time or recurring services. The post covers implementation steps, subscription management features, and fraud prevention techniques to help developers generate revenue while maintaining customer trust. It also addresses minimizing payment disputes and handling subscription lifecycle management for Canva app developers.
